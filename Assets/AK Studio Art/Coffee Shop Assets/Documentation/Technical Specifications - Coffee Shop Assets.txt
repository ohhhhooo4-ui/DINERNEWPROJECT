Technical Specifications - Coffee Shop Assets
Author: AK Studio Art 

=== Summary ===
Coffee Shop Assets is a complete 3D pack for Unity featuring coffee
machines, food, drinks, furniture, and accessories to build realistic and
stylish coffee shops.
 
=== Content ===
- Number of Unique Meshes: 97.
- Number of Unique Materials: 81.
- Number of polygons: 2 to 23.134.
- Texture sizes used:
  - 2048x2048 (117)
  - 1024x1024 (29)
  - 256x256 (2)
  - 512x512 (2)
- Types of texture maps: BaseMap, Emissive, MaskMap, NormalMap.

=== Statistics ===
The package includes:
  - 46 FBX models.
  - 106 Prefabs.
  - 81 Materials.
  - 150 Textures.
  - 1 Demo Scene(s) with preconfigured Lightmap.

=== Notes ===
- Optimized 3D Models for performance.
- VR-ready assets.
- Baked GI included.
- Post Processing Setup provided.
